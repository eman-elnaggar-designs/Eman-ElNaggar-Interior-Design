02 — The Designer

Designing with Intention

Eman El-Naggar is an Alexandria-based interior designer whose career spans more than two decades, with a practice established in 1998 and projects extending across Egypt. Her approach to design is rooted in timeless elegance, thoughtful proportion, and a deep understanding of how people experience space. Rather than following trends, Eman creates interiors that balance architectural character with functionality, refined materials, and carefully considered details.

From private residences and luxury apartments to villas and bespoke interiors, every project is approached with the same attention to detail — from the initial concept and spatial planning through material selection, custom furnishings, styling, and final execution. Based in Alexandria and working across Egypt, Eman personally oversees each project to ensure that the original vision is carried through to every detail.

— Eman El-Naggar
